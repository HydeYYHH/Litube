An unofficial, open-source YouTube client for Android.

<b>Features</b>
* <b>Browse YouTube.</b> Explore your home feed, search for videos, and browse channels, playlists, Shorts, and your library.
* <b>Play your way.</b> Enjoy ad-free playback with controls for video quality, speed, subtitles, and audio tracks. Keep listening in the background, use picture-in-picture or the mini-player, or cast to another device.
* <b>Download for later.</b> Save videos, audio, subtitles, or entire playlists. Choose the quality and language, and pause or resume downloads as needed.
* <b>Keep things organized.</b> Build queues with shuffle, repeat, and autoplay. Watch history and Watch later are available even when you're signed out.
* <b>Multiple accounts, all in one.</b> Easily switch between your anonymous account and multiple signed-in accounts.
* <b>Make it yours.</b> Customize SponsorBlock, playback gestures, themes, and language. Enable DeArrow for alternative titles and thumbnails.
